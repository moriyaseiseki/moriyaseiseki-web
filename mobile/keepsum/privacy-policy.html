KeepSum Privacy Policy

Version 1.1 • Effective Date: November 18, 2026 • For KeepSum 1.0.0 (Android)

1. Summary

KeepSum is an Android app that summarizes CSV files on your device. It does not collect, send, or share any data. It has no internet permission, no account, no ads, no analytics, and no AI. The files you open are read and summarized on your device only.

2. Who Is Responsible

KeepSum is made by MORIYA SEISEKI (杜屋整積), Japan. This notice describes what KeepSum does with data. It applies in addition to our general Privacy Policy; where the two differ, this notice applies to KeepSum.

3. Your Files

  • KeepSum reads a file only when you open it: with the app’s file picker, or with “Open with” from another app.
  • The file is read and summarized on your device. It is never uploaded, copied elsewhere, or changed; KeepSum only reads it.
  • So that the file can reopen after Android closes the app in the background, Android keeps KeepSum’s permission to read the last file you opened with the picker, and the app keeps that file’s location and your filters on the device. Opening another file releases the previous permission. Nothing of this leaves your device.

4. Data Collected and Shared

None. KeepSum does not collect personal data or any other data, and does not share data with anyone. It contains no advertising, analytics, or tracking libraries, and it uses no AI service.

5. Permissions and Internet

KeepSum requests no Android permissions, including no internet access, so it cannot send anything off your device. The links on its About screen (this page and KeepSum’s website) open in your web browser, which is a separate app; visiting our website is covered by our general Privacy Policy.

6. Backup and Device Transfer

KeepSum turns off Android’s backup and device-to-device transfer for its data on every supported Android version (Android 10 and later). Its data is not saved to your Google account’s backup and is not copied to a new phone when you move to one.

7. Logs and Crash Reports

  • KeepSum writes nothing from your files to the device log: no file names, column names, values, or search text.
  • If KeepSum crashes, Android and, if you allow usage and diagnostics sharing on your device, Google Play record a technical report: where in the code it happened. In rare cases it may include a short message from Android or from a library that KeepSum uses, which KeepSum cannot control.
  • Android itself may log that an app opened a file. That is the system’s log, not KeepSum’s.
  • The device log can be read only through USB debugging, or in a bug report that you make and choose to share. Other apps cannot read KeepSum’s log lines.

8. Google Play

KeepSum is distributed through Google Play. Google processes data about your use of Google Play and your device, including crash and performance statistics (Android vitals) if you allow usage and diagnostics sharing, under Google’s Privacy Policy. We see these statistics and crash reports in Google Play Console; they contain no data collected by KeepSum, because KeepSum collects none.

9. Children

KeepSum is intended for users aged 13 and over. It collects no data from anyone, including children.

10. Your Choices

Because KeepSum keeps nothing about you outside your device, the app gives us no data to access, correct, or delete. Uninstalling KeepSum removes everything it stored on your device. If you have emailed us, you can ask us to see or delete your messages at any time (Section 12). For any question, contact us (Section 12).

11. Changes to This Notice

If KeepSum’s handling of data changes, we update this notice before the new version is released, with a new effective date and an entry below.

  • Version 1.0 (November 17, 2026): the first version, for KeepSum 1.0.0.
