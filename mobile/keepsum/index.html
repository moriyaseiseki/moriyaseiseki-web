KeepSum

Private CSV stats, on your Android device

KeepSum gives you a quick profile of a CSV file, right on your phone: before formal analysis, before a pipeline, or just to check what a file holds. See each column’s type and statistics, scroll and filter the table, and draw charts.

Overview

Rows, columns, how complete the data is, and each column’s statistics: count, missing, distinct, min, max, mean, median, standard deviation, most common values.

Explorer

The whole table with the header row in view, filtered by text or by a number range.

Charts

Histograms and line charts for numbers; bar charts of the most common values for text.

Private by Design

  • On your device only: files are read and summarized on your phone, never uploaded.
  • No internet: KeepSum has no internet permission, so it can’t send anything anywhere.
  • No account: no sign-in, no ads, no analytics.
  • No AI: your data isn’t sent to, or read by, any AI service.
